About

The work around AI.

Wadent is an early-stage company exploring how regulated financial-services teams can use AI in sensitive workflows without giving up control, accountability, or the ability to explain what happened.

We are focused on the work around AI: human review, operational exceptions, evidence, controlled access, and practical deployment choices.

We believe AI should make teams more capable—not make responsibility disappear.

Founder

Aaliyah

Aaliyah founded Awynn LLC, which builds Wadent. She studied information science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and spent the early part of her career in technology consulting.

The consulting work is where the questions started. Moving between client systems, she kept meeting the same situation: operations running on legacy infrastructure, and organizations that knew it was costing them without a clear sense of where to begin. To understand one corner of it properly, she built a working prototype that walks through reconciliation exceptions and the reasoning behind each one.

She is now talking with people working in finance and operations to test that read against their day-to-day, and working alongside a student researcher to build the domain depth the problem asks for.
