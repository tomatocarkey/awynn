Founder

Aaliyah

Aaliyah founded Awynn LLC. She studied information science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and spent the early part of her career in technology consulting.

The consulting work is where the company started. Moving between client systems, she kept meeting the same situation: financial operations running on legacy infrastructure, and organizations that knew it was costing them without a clear sense of where to begin. To understand one corner of it properly — hedge fund operations — she built a triage tool for trade reconciliation breaks, working through how a trade support desk decides what a break is, why it happened, and what is genuinely at risk.

The tool answered the small question and raised a larger one. What had looked like a scatter of isolated frustrations turned out to be structural. She began talking with people working in finance to test that read against their day-to-day, and is now working alongside a student researcher to build the domain depth the problem asks for.

Awynn LLC is early, and deliberately quiet about specifics. The problem is not a quiet one.
