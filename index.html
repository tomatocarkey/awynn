The problem

AI creates work around the work.

AI can summarize, classify, analyze, and recommend. But in high-stakes workflows, teams still need to answer:

  • What information did the system use?
  • What did it recommend or change?
  • Who reviewed it?
  • What happened when something did not look right?
  • Can we explain the outcome later?

Wadent is exploring how to make those answers clear.

Example workflow

From recommendation to accountable outcome.

  1. 01

    An AI workflow identifies an incomplete or inconsistent item.

  2. 02

    The system gathers the relevant context and suggests a next step.

  3. 03

    A human reviews, approves, rejects, or changes the suggestion.

  4. 04

    The issue is assigned, tracked, and resolved.

  5. 05

    The final record shows what happened and who was responsible.

For investment operations, this could include reviewing an AI-supported portfolio recommendation, a trade-related exception, or a data discrepancy across approved records.
