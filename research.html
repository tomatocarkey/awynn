Customer discovery

What we are trying to learn.

Wadent is an early-stage hypothesis, not a finished product. We are talking with operations, technology, risk, compliance, and investment leaders about how AI is actually used in sensitive workflows — and what breaks when it is.

Open questions

The questions we are asking.

  • Where does AI-assisted work already need a human owner, and who is that today?
  • What evidence do teams need to reconstruct a decision months later?
  • How do incomplete or inconsistent items get chased, tracked, and closed now?
  • What has to be true about data access before security and legal sign off?
  • Which deployment arrangements are genuinely required, and which are assumed?

We are not assuming these problems are proven in every organization. That is what the conversations are for.

Workflow example

An illustrative workflow demonstration.

Illustrative workflow — research demonstration only. This example shows how a system-generated recommendation can be reviewed by a person, checked against simple rules, and recorded as an accountable outcome. It does not involve live data, live trading, investment advice, customer funds, or performance claims.

The demonstration walks through reconciliation exceptions: a set of items where records disagree, the reasoning assembled for each one, and the point at which a person approves, changes, or rejects the suggested next step.

It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is submitted, stored, or sent anywhere.
